ETHICO-LEGAL_SYSTEM
│
├── 1. ENTITIES (Core Components)
│   ├── HUMAN_BEING (rights-holder)
│   ├── GOVERNMENT_INSTITUTIONS (duty-bearer)
│   ├── LEGAL_AUTHORITIES (judges, lawmakers)
│   ├── CIVIL_SOCIETY (NGOs, media, activists)
│   └── INTERNATIONAL_BODIES (UN, ICC, ICJ)
│
├── 2. INPUTS – What Feeds the System?
│   ├── MORAL_VALUES → dignity, justice, equality
│   ├── HUMAN_RIGHTS_DECLARATIONS → UDHR, treaties
│   ├── SOCIAL_NORMS → cultural views on fairness
│   ├── CRIMES_AND_VIOLATIONS → triggers enforcement
│   ├── PUBLIC_DEMAND_FOR_JUSTICE → protests, petitions
│   └── EVIDENCE_AND_TESTIMONY → basis for legal decisions
│
├── 3. OUTPUTS – What Results from the System?
│   ├── LEGAL_PROTECTIONS → laws that safeguard rights
│   ├── JUDGMENTS_AND_RULINGS → court decisions
│   ├── POLICY_REFORMS → new laws based on ethical review
│   ├── DETERRENCE_EFFECT → reduced crime due to fear of punishment
│   ├── ACCOUNTABILITY_MECHANISMS → holding power to account
│   └── SOCIAL_LEGITIMACY → public trust in institutions
│
├── 4. STATES – Functional Modes of the System
│   ├── FUNCTIONAL_AND_FAIR → justice is accessible and impartial
│   ├── FRAGILE → weak institutions, corruption
│   ├── COLLAPSING → rule of law broken (e.g., war zones)
│   ├── REFORMING → undergoing change after crisis
│   ├── RESILIENT → self-correcting through oversight
│   └── AUTHORITARIAN_DISTORTION → used to suppress dissent
│
├── 5. MODIFIERS – What Changes Its Behavior?
│   ├── QUALITY_OF_GOVERNANCE → transparency vs. corruption
│   ├── LEVEL_OF_CIVIC_AWARENESS → public understanding of rights
│   ├── EXTERNAL_PRESSURE → sanctions, diplomacy
│   ├── HISTORICAL_TRAUMA → legacy of colonialism, conflict
│   ├── RELIGIOUS_OR_IDEOLOGICAL_FRAMES → shape moral views
│   └── TECHNOLOGICAL_CHANGE → digital evidence, AI in courts
│
├── 6. PROCESSES – How the System Functions
│   ├── NORM_GENERATION → creating shared values ("torture is wrong")
│   ├── LAW_MAKING → legislative process
│   ├── ADJUDICATION → interpreting and applying law
│   ├── ENFORCEMENT → police, international tribunals
│   ├── FEEDBACK_LOOP → appeals, reviews, public scrutiny
│   ├── ACCOUNTABILITY_CYCLE → investigation → trial → reform
│   ├── HUMAN_RIGHTS_MONITORING → reporting violations
│   └── LEGITIMACY_REINFORCEMENT → restoring trust after abuse
│
├── 7. DEPENDENCIES – What the System Relies On
│   ├── INDEPENDENT_JUDICIARY
│   ├── FREE_AND_FAIR_MEDIA
│   ├── CIVIC_EDUCATION
│   ├── PROTECTION_OF_WHISTLEBLOWERS
│   ├── ACCESS_TO_COURTS
│   └── PEACE_AND_STABILITY
│
├── 8. BOUNDARIES – Where Does It Begin/End?
│   ├── MORAL_vs._LEGAL_DOMAIN → not all wrongs are illegal
│   ├── NATIONAL_LAW_vs._INTERNATIONAL_LAW
│   ├── INDIVIDUAL_RIGHTS_vs._COLLECTIVE_SECURITY
│   ├── PRIVATE_LIFE_vs._PUBLIC_ACCOUNTABILITY
│   └── JUSTICE_AS_CORRECTION_vs._JUSTICE_AS_PREVENTION
│
├── 9. REPOSITORIES – Where Is Memory Stored?
│   ├── CONSTITUTIONS_AND_CHARTERS
│   ├── COURT_RECORDS_AND_PRECEDENTS
│   ├── HUMAN_RIGHTS_REPORTS (Amnesty, HRW)
│   ├── INTERNATIONAL_TREATIES (ICESCR, ICCPR)
│   ├── LEGAL_CODEXES (civil, criminal law)
│   └── CULTURAL_MEMORY (oral histories, trauma archives)
│
├── 10. GOALS – Why Does This System Exist?
│   ├── PROTECT_HUMAN_DIGNITY
│   ├── SAFEGUARD_THE_RIGHT_TO_LIFE
│   ├── ENSURE_EQUALITY_BEFORE_THE_LAW
│   ├── PREVENT_ARBITRARY_POWER
│   ├── PROMOTE_SOCIAL_JUSTICE
│   └── RESTORE_TRUST_IN_INSTITUTIONS
│
└── 11. BEHAVIORS – Emergent Dynamics
    ├── BALANCE_ORIENTED → weighs freedom vs. security
    ├── CHANGE_ORIENTED → evolves with society (e.g., LGBTQ+ rights)
    ├── INTEGRATION_ORIENTED → links ethics, law, culture
    ├── FEEDBACK_CONTROLLED → self-corrects via appeals, oversight
    └── RESILIENCE_ORIENTED → recovers after injustice or collapse
