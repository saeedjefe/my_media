CHANGE-OF-STATE VERB TAXONOMY (with Neuroscientific States)

1. PHYSICAL
  1.1 Phase: melt, freeze, thaw, evaporate, condense, crystallize, solidify
  1.2 Shape/Form: grow, shrink, expand, contract, fold, flatten, inflate, deflate, stretch, compress
  1.3 Integrity: break, shatter, crack, split, tear, crush, erode, corrode, rust
  1.4 Appearance: darken, brighten, redden, yellow, dim, fade, blur, sharpen, polish
  1.5 Chemical/Energetic: oxidize, ignite, burn, cool, heat, deactivate, activate

2. BIOLOGICAL (non-neural)
  2.1 Growth/Development: mature, age, develop, bloom, wither, decay, rot
  2.2 Health: heal, recover, convalesce, deteriorate, weaken, strengthen, sicken, infect

3. NEUROSCIENTIFIC / PHYSIOLOGICAL-COGNITIVE STATES
  3.1 Consciousness/Arousal: wake, wake up, fall asleep, rouse, faint, pass out, revive, sober up
  3.2 Attention/Focus: attend, focus, defocus, habituate, sensitize
  3.3 Memory/Encoding: learn, remember, forget, consolidate (memory), retrieve
  3.4 Perception/Threshold: notice, detect, perceive, adapt
  3.5 Valence/Affect (neuro-affective): calm, excite, depress, arouse (affective)

4. COGNITIVE (belief/knowledge)
  realize, discover, understand, misinterpret, reassess, infer, deduce, recognize

5. AFFECTIVE (emotion)
  anger, sadden, cheer (up), frighten, annoy, frustrate, amuse, calm (down)

6. SOCIAL/ROLE/STATUS
  marry, divorce, promote, demote, elect, appoint, knight, convict, pardon, inherit, forfeit

7. ECONOMIC/VALUATION
  appreciate, depreciate, cheapen, rise, fall, inflate, deflate

8. INFORMATIONAL/REPRESENTATION
  encode, decode, digitize, translate, categorize, reclassify, rename, relabel, reveal, expose, redact

9. LOCATIVE/SPATIAL
  arrive, depart, enter, exit, stand up, sit down, rise, descend, relocate, align, rotate, invert

10. NORMATIVE/DEONTIC
  permit, forbid, require, authorize, ban
