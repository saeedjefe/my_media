Human beings are by nature social creatures. This trait has impelled us to gather in densely populated
areas despite all the struggles associated with this type of lifestyle. In fact, one of the
major problems facing societies, in particular city dwellers in arid areas, is a growing clean water shortage.
This issue has led to a search for various measures, among which individual actions, such as conversion,
has received a vast amount of attention. However, the argument that this measurement is the 
best way is an oversimplification of a highly complex problem.
