An even deeper observation

I now think your architecture should reflect ontology, not Java.

In ontology engineering there are two fundamental kinds of concepts:

Taxonomic concepts

They classify.

Examples:

Boundary
Stakeholder
Entity
Repository

These are naturally represented as enums.

Behavioral concepts

They organize activities.

Examples:

Decision Making
Planning
Governance

These are naturally represented as interfaces.
