Term 1 Syllabus

Session 1
Textbook: Understanding and Using English Grammar, Ch. 16, pp. 357–359

Objectives:
• Identify parts of speech (noun phrases, verb phrases, and prepositional phrases)
• Distinguish transitive vs. intransitive action verbs and linking verbs
• Construct parallel structures
• Apply correct comma usage in coordination and series

Assignment: pp. 360–361

Session 2
Textbook: Understanding and Using English Grammar, Ch. 16, p. 362; Ch. 17, pp. 370–371

Objectives:
• Connect two independent clauses to form compound sentences
• Distinguish independent clauses from dependent clauses (adverb clauses)
• Apply comma conventions between clauses
• Examine functions and taxonomy of subordinating conjunctions

Assignment: pp. 363, 372

Session 3
Textbook: Understanding and Using English Grammar, Ch. 12, pp. 247–250, 253–254

Objectives:
• Identify noun clauses and analyze their nominal functions
• Use noun clauses in written and spoken contexts

Assignment: pp. 251–252, 255

Session 4
Textbook: Understanding and Using English Grammar, Ch. 17, pp. 272–273, 276, 278, 280, 282, 284, 287–288, 292–293

Objectives:
• Identify and use adjective clauses based on the nominal functions of relative pronouns

Assignment: pp. 272–294

Sessions 5–8

Textbooks:
• Focus on Vocabulary, Unit 1, Ch. 1
• English Grammar, pp. 347, 257, 295, 241

Objectives:
• Identify clause types using color-coding and recognize reduced adjective clauses (appositive phrases)
• Differentiate adverbial, nominal, and adjectival functions introduced by prepositional, verb, and noun phrases
• Distinguish verbs of perception from verbs of cognition
• Analyze six categories of emotional meaning
• Distinguish object-experiencer emotional psych causatives vs. subject-experiencer emotional state verbs
• Differentiate participial adjectives (-ed vs. -ing)
• Identify complements of adjectives and abstract noun phrases
• Introduce nominalization

Sessions 9–10
Textbook: Understanding and Using English Grammar
• Ch. 17, pp. 373, 378–379, 381–382, 384, 386–388
• Ch. 18 (reduction of adverb clauses)
• Ch. 12, p. 256 (reduction of noun clauses)
