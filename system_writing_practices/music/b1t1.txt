There are different types of music in the world today. Why do we need music? Is traditional music of
a country is more important than the international music that is heard everywhere?

Music is a form of artistic expression whose influence on human beings is so profound that it is difficult to
imagine a social setting in which it plays no role. This is because music shapes our cognitive, emotional, and
physiological states while also influencing the social interactions through which relationships are formed and
collective identities are reinforced. Nevertheless, although traditional music can help preserve a country's
cultural heritage and strengthen a shared sense of identity, the growing dominance of international music may
diminish people's engagement with their musical traditions, potentially weakening an already fragile sense of
national identity.
