Below is a comprehensive list of official Judo techniques recognized by the Kodokan, organized by major categories. Most come from the Kodokan classification of 68 Nage‑waza (throws) and 32 Katame‑waza (grappling) currently recognized. Some variations are included where widely accepted in modern practice and Kodokan references.
